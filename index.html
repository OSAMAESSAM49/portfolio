01 / About me

More Than One Profession. One Creative Mind.

I’m Osama Essam, a dentist with a passion for creativity, visual communication, and technology.

I graduated from Mansoura University in 2017 and run my own practice as the owner of Gannah Dental Clinic. But my journey goes beyond the dental chair. I’ve been designing since 2013 and filming since 2023 — and today I move between clinical dentistry, graphic design, dental photography, video, and AI-powered creative production to turn ideas into meaningful experiences.

I believe creativity is not limited to one profession. It’s a mindset that influences everything I do.

“A visionary dentist blending artistry and science — crafting smiles by day and captivating stories by night.”
